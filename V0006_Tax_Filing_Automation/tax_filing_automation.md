Here is the step-by-step breakdown for the four schedules shown on your screen.

---

### Step 1: Schedule CYLA (Current Year's Loss Adjustment)

This is an automated system schedule that applies tax set-off rules to offset any losses incurred during FY 2025–26 against this year's taxable income.

* **File/PDF to Check:** Your **Broker Capital Gains Tax P&L Statement**.
* **Fields to Check in the File:**
* Go to the **P&L Summary / Net Realized Capital Gains** section.
* Look for **Short-Term Capital Loss (STCL)** and **Long-Term Capital Loss (LTCL)** incurred in FY 2025–26.


* **What to Check on the Portal:**
* Click open **Schedule CYLA**.
* Verify that current-year losses are automatically set off against gains (e.g., STCL set off against STCG/LTCG, and LTCL set off only against LTCG).
* Confirm the net taxable capital gain figure matches the net figure after loss adjustment on your broker statement. Click **Confirm**.



---

### Step 2: Schedule BFLA (Brought Forward Loss Adjustment)

This schedule sets off unadjusted capital losses carried forward from **previous assessment years (AY 2025–26, AY 2024–25, etc.)** against this year's gains.

* **File/PDF to Check:** Your **Previous Year’s Filed ITR Form/Acknowledgement (AY 2025–26)** or the portal's Loss Matrix (`e-File > Income Tax Returns > View Filed Returns`).
* **Fields to Check in the File:**
* Open last year's ITR PDF and scroll to **Schedule CFL**.
* Check the values under **Brought Forward STCL** or **Brought Forward LTCL**.


* **What to Check on the Portal:**
* Click open **Schedule BFLA**.
* Ensure the pre-filled brought forward loss figures match what was carried forward in your previous year's filed return.
* *(Note: If you had no losses in previous years, all entries here will show ₹0).* Click **Confirm**.



---

### Step 3: Schedule CFL (Details of Losses to be Carried Forward)

This schedule records any remaining losses from the current year (or previous years) that could not be fully set off, saving them to be carried forward for up to 8 future assessment years.

* **File/PDF to Check:** Your **Broker Capital Gains Tax P&L Statement**.
* **Fields to Check in the File:**
* Look at the **Net Realized Loss / Unadjusted Loss** at the bottom of the summary table.


* **What to Check on the Portal:**
* Click open **Schedule CFL**.
* Find the row labeled **AY 2026–27**.
* Verify that any remaining STCL or LTCL for this year is shown under the **"Loss remaining for carry forward"** column. Click **Confirm**.



---

### Step 4: Schedule SI (Income Chargeable to Tax at Special Rates)

Capital gains (and virtual digital assets) are taxed at special fixed rates (e.g., Section 111A for short-term equity, Section 112A for long-term equity) rather than your normal slab rates.

* **File/PDF to Check:** Your **Broker Capital Gains Tax P&L Statement**.
* **Fields to Check in the File:**
* Look at section-wise tax classifications:
* **Section 111A:** STCG on equity shares / equity mutual funds.
* **Section 112A:** LTCG on equity shares / equity mutual funds.
* **Section 112:** LTCG on debt funds, unlisted securities, or property.




* **What to Check on the Portal:**
* Click open **Schedule SI**.
* Verify that the income listed under **Section 111A** and **Section 112A** matches your post-set-off capital gain figures from Schedules CYLA/BFLA. Click **Confirm**.
